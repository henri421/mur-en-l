L-shaped retaining wall

Stability and sections of an L-shaped retaining wall, Eurocodes of both generations.

The recommended values of the standards are used, in the absence of a Belgian national annex. Check these values before any use on a real structure.

A strip is a distributed load of finite width, defined by its distance from the back of the wall and its width. What rests on the heel weighs on it, the rest pushes the virtual screen. Permanent loads are denoted G and variable loads Q.

Out of scope

This tool does not cover groundwater, the shear key, sloping ground, backfill cohesion, passive resistance in front of the toe, undrained analysis, serviceability states (cracking) and overall site stability.

Without a national annex, the recommended values of the standards are used.
